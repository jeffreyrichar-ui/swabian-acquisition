§ 02b, Sourcing infrastructure

A sourcing engine, not a Rolodex.

Most buyers are a partner, two associates, and an inbox. Swabian is one operator running a system he engineered himself: a Python pipeline orchestrating Claude for qualification, enrichment, and outreach drafting against a continuously refreshed universe of fitting companies.

Practically, that means when Jeff calls, he already knows the shape of your business. He's read what's public, mapped your competitive set, and run the numbers he can run. The first conversation skips the introductions and starts where it should, at fit.

For owners and advisors, the upshot is simpler: a buyer who is already prepared, who can move on real timing instead of a calendar quarter, and who isn't waiting on someone else to read the file.

§ 04, Process

First call to clean close.

A confidential conversation.

30 minutes. NDA in advance if you'd like one. We discuss the business at a high level. No diligence requests, no document demands. You leave knowing whether there's a real fit. If not, we say so directly.
